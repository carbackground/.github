# carbackground.ai

**carbackground.ai** (Car Background AI, formerly CarBG) is an AI car photo editor for car dealers. It turns ordinary photos taken on a lot or a street into studio and showroom listing photos, ready for dealer websites, marketplaces, social posts and ads.

- Website: https://carbackground.ai
- App: https://app.carbackground.ai
- Contact: hello@carbackground.ai

## What it does

- **Background replacement.** Puts the car in a studio, showroom or outdoor scene. The car is kept as photographed (same car, wheels, badges and viewing angle) and gets shadows, lighting and reflections that match the new scene.
- **Custom backgrounds.** Creates a scene of the dealer's own, such as their real showroom or brand colours, from a description or reference photos.
- **License plates.** Blanks or blurs the plate, or replaces it with the dealer's plate logo.
- **Dealer branding.** Adds the dealer's logo to every photo.
- **Marketplace formats.** Reframes photos to the aspect ratio a marketplace asks for.
- **Whole listings.** Exterior, interior and detail shots get one consistent look, and whole inventories can be processed in bulk.

## Who uses it

Used-car dealers and dealer groups, automotive marketplaces, car photographers, agencies and private sellers. The app is available in 24 languages.
